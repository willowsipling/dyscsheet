About dyscsheet

dyscsheet is a statistics worksheet app made for people with dyscalculia (like me!). It works like graph paper, which is one strategy that's helpful for folks with dyscalculia to keep numbers and formulae aligned. Every letter and symbol gets its own boxed, and so learners can see everything spaced and notated evently and orderly!

Roots, fractions, and the other mathematical notations are drawn right on the grid, so your work looks the way it would on paper. When you're done, you can turn your grid into a PDF, or output your finished work into typeset LaTeX/MathJax, ready to paste into notes apps like Obsidian.

Every button has an exaplanation, too. Hover on a button and the info strip under the toolbar says what it does or what a symbol means, and its keyboard shortcut (if applicable). The reference card in the Documentation collects all of that in one place.
