About dyscsheet

dyscsheet is a maths worksheet made for people with dyscalculia. It works like graph paper: every number, letter and symbol sits in its own box, so columns stay lined up and nothing drifts out of place.

Roots, fractions and the other structures are drawn right on the grid, so your work looks the way it would on paper. When you're done, it can turn your work into typeset LaTeX/MathJax, ready to paste into notes apps like Obsidian.

Every button explains itself: point at it or tab to it and the strip under the toolbar says what it does, what a symbol means, and its keyboard shortcut. The reference card collects all of that in one place.
