How to use dyscsheet

try your best for now??

the "Finished Math" button shows the pretty LaTeX version of your work and also a MathJax option for putting it directly into obsidian (you'll need to add double $$ though)

How to make room inside a root or fraction

1. Type the extra bits you want to add to the end of the root. Then, click and drag selecting from the root's root, and then press the root button on the GUI to include it. It will extend the root, instead of making a double root now.

2. "Add column left/right" from inside the root extends the root.

Tidy fraction

If you copy a fraction from the line above and change parts of it, it can end up with leftover blank spaces or a Long fraction bar. Select the junk cells of the leftover fraction, then press "tidy fraction" in the Wrap bar (right after the fraction button). you may have to make judicious use of the undo lol. NOTE: it can't tidy a fraction that has another fraction in its top. Sorry
